SawDust

Design • Plan • Build

A woodworking design tool for Windows. Draw a piece as boards, panels and joints; take the cut list, sheet layout, shop drawings and labels to the bench.

Get it on the Microsoft Store Watch the tutorial

30-day, full-featured free trial. No subscription.

What you get from one design

  • Cut list with costs Every part, final size, rough size and board feet, priced from your own material list.
  • Sheet layouts Plywood parts nested onto sheets, with the offcuts, as a drawing and a DXF.
  • Shop drawings Dimensioned views of each assembly, printable or as PDF.
  • Joinery that checks itself Dados, rabbets, pocket screws, dowels and more. SawDust will not let you draw a joint that cannot be cut.
  • Parameters Name a few measurements and build on them; resize the whole piece by changing one number.
  • CNC and labels DXF files for a router, assembly steps, and part labels for the stack at the bench.

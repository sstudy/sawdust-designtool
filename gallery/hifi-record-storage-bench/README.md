# Hi-Fi Record Storage Bench

A long, low walnut-plywood cabinet with two sliding doors, five internal dividers for LP storage, a cushioned seat on top and six tapered solid-walnut feet. About 80" wide, 16" deep and knee height. Turntable and amplifier sit on the open end; records file between the dividers.

**Files**

- `hifi-record-storage-bench.swdt` — the design. Open it with File → Open in SawDust.
- `bench.png` — the viewport view.

**What it demonstrates**

- A carcass in 3/4" walnut plywood with a 1/4" plywood back in grooves, and solid banding on every visible plywood edge (the `T Front Banding`, `LS Front Banding`… parts).
- Dividers housed in dados, sized from `CarcaseDepth`/`CarcaseHeight` parameters so the whole bench resizes from a few numbers.
- Sliding door panels running in top and bottom grooves of different depths (`DoorGrooveDepthTop` deeper than `DoorGrooveDepthBottom`, so a door lifts in and drops down).
- Tapered feet cut with `taper` features, and mitred banding corners with `miterCut`.
- A cut list that separates sheet goods from solid stock and prices the plywood by the sheet.

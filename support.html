Support

Bugs, questions and ideas

Report a bug or suggest a feature

Issues are tracked in the open on GitHub, so you can see what has already been reported and follow progress on your own report. A free GitHub account is needed to post.

Open a new issue · Browse existing issues

Email

If you would rather not use GitHub, or your question involves one of your own designs, write to sawdust.designtool@outlook.com.

What helps most in a bug report

  • The SawDust version, from Help → About SawDust.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see.
  • For a crash or an error message, the diagnostic log. It is at %LOCALAPPDATA%\SawDust\sawdust.log and stays on your computer until you choose to share it. It can contain file names and paths, so glance through it first.
  • The .swdt file, if the problem is specific to one design and you are happy to share it. Email is the better route for that.

Before reporting

The user manual is built into the app under Help → User Manual, and Help → Keyboard Shortcuts lists every gesture. Many "is this a bug?" questions are answered there.
